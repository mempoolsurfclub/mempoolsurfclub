## Complete inventory

### Protocols & Metaprotocols

- **Ordinal Theory & Inscriptions** — `ACTIVE` — `ordinal-theory-inscriptions` — Core Ordinals — Casey Rodarmor’s ordinal theory and inscription system for tracking individual satoshis and attaching digital artifacts to Bitcoin transactions.
- **BRC-20** — `ACTIVE` — `brc-20` — Fungible-token experiments — An experimental fungible-token convention introduced by the pseudonymous domo using Ordinals inscriptions for deploy, mint, and transfer operations.
- **Bitmap** — `ACTIVE` — `bitmap` — Block-data / spatial standards — A metaprotocol that lets users inscribe Bitcoin block-height identifiers such as `840000.bitmap` to claim abstract digital districts based on Bitcoin blocks.
- **Digital Matter Theory (DMT)** — `ACTIVE` — `digital-matter-theory` — Block-data / non-arbitrary assets — A framework for deriving digital assets and traits from patterns already present in Bitcoin block data rather than assigning arbitrary off-chain rarity.
- **Non-Arbitrary Tokens (NAT)** — `ACTIVE` — `non-arbitrary-tokens` — Block-data / non-arbitrary assets — Digital Matter Theory token conventions that derive supply or traits from deterministic Bitcoin block-data patterns.
- **TAP Protocol** — `ACTIVE` — `tap-protocol` — Programmable inscription metaprotocols — An Ordinals-based metaprotocol that extends BRC-20-style assets with additional transfer, trading, authority, DMT, and Bitmap-oriented capabilities.
- **GeneratOrd** — `UNCERTAIN` — `generatord` — Recursive collection tooling — A recursive/generative Ordinals framework created by the Fomojis team to compose collection assets from shared on-chain inscription resources.
- **RSIC Metaprotocol** — `HISTORICAL` — `rsic-metaprotocol` — Pre-Runes distribution experiments — A 21,000-piece Ordinals distribution experiment that gamified a pre-Runes 'mining' phase and later connected holders to RSIC•GENESIS•RUNE.
