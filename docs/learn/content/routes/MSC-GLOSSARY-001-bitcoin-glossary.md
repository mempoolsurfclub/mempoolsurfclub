# Bitcoin Glossary

## 1. Introductory deck

Definitions identify the concept and its important boundary without replacing the guide, hub, path, or route that owns the subject.

## 2. Full destination copy

Bitcoin vocabulary can move between protocol rules, wallet behavior, node policy, software implementations, companies, markets, and emerging systems. A useful short definition tells the reader which kind of thing a term names and where the complete explanation lives.

Each entry has one preferred term, one concise definition, and one canonical editorial destination. Topic guides own focused long-form subjects. Category hubs own orientation across stable subject areas. The featured route connects several canonical destinations. The glossary owns concise preferred definitions and no duplicate long-form articles.

Read each definition as a boundary rather than a complete lesson. Planning handles are not public URLs. Every term link, search result, letter control, category link, and return action remains inactive until a separate implementation and publication workflow confirms the destination.

Stable concepts such as input, output, UTXO, private key, block, confirmation, and proof of work should remain synchronized with their canonical guides. Software, provider, market, legal, ecosystem, and proposal terms require publication-time renewal because behavior, scope, status, and terminology can change.

OP_CAT remains a proposed tapscript opcode requiring consensus change before activation. BRC-20 remains an experimental convention interpreted by off-chain indexers. Ark, BitVM, RGB, Runes, and related systems are described by current specifications and implementation evidence, not promotional labels. A BIP, paper, repository, or implementation does not by itself prove activation, deployment, adoption, security, or suitability.

`Self-Custody` and `Open-Source` are the preferred glossary labels. In prose, capitalization follows sentence context, and `open-source` remains the adjective.

The glossary does not certify any wallet, provider, protocol, company, project, or practice. It does not provide personalized financial, legal, tax, security, or custody advice. It does not turn software popularity into protocol authority or convert a planned handle into a published page.

Final review aligned Bitcoin Core with its accepted canonical guide: `An open-source Bitcoin node implementation and software project.` The initial planning map's mutable adoption ranking was not carried into the copy-locked package.

The return destination is `MSC-LRN-HOME | Learn`. Readers may continue through categories, paths, the featured route, or another term without changing canonical subject ownership.
